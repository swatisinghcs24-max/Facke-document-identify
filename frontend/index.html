AI-Based Fake Identity & Document Screening System

AI-assisted identity and document verification prototype providing first-level automated screening across passports, national IDs, and visas with explainable multi-signal risk analysis.

Start Screening Open Admin Dashboard
Pipeline Architecture

Automated Verification Flow

01. Ingestion & Quality

Validates image formats, resolution thresholds, Laplacian blur variance, and exposure balance to ensure forensic admissibility.

02. OCR & Field Extraction

Executes EasyOCR text recognition to extract document holder name, DOB, document number, nationality, and expiry validity.

03. Tampering Analysis

Heuristic OpenCV spatial edge density, local noise variance, and compression boundary anomaly inspection for splicing cues.

04. Face & Risk Synthesis

Correlates document portrait against applicant selfie and computes a normalized 0–100 risk score with explainable reasoning.

Key Prototype Capabilities

  • ✓ Multi-format support: Passports, Visas, and National ID Cards
  • ✓ Optical quality inspection (sharpness, brightness, contrast)
  • ✓ Expiry date detection against real-time clock
  • ✓ Missing mandatory field checklist verification
  • ✓ OpenCV edge gradient & noise inconsistency tampering flags
  • ✓ Biometric face detection & similarity comparison
  • ✓ Downloadable formal audit report via ReportLab
